Kafka Outbound Backpressure & Workflow Isolation

A memory-aware backpressure mechanism that keeps the JVM stable during high-volume Kafka outbound processing, especially with large payloads.

  • Byte-based admission control with estimated message footprints, in-flight byte budgets and concurrency limits.
  • Adaptive concurrency with AIMD, driven by JVM heap utilization, processing errors and system health.
  • Workflow-level isolation by workflowId and stageId, so a slow integration cannot affect unrelated workflows.
  • Load tested across payload sizes and concurrency levels, tuning heap and Kubernetes pod memory.
JavaKafkaAIMDKubernetesLoad testing
